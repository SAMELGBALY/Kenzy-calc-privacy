Privacy Policy — Kenzy Calc

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Kenzy Calc is an educational scientific calculator for school students and other users. It includes interfaces for primary, preparatory, and secondary study stages and may be used by children and adults.

Information stored on your device

The app stores calculation history locally on your device. It is not sent to the developer's servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its application data may delete this information.

Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, app and device details, and ad interaction data to provide and measure ads, subject to applicable settings and requirements. Before an ad request, the app applies child-directed age-restricted treatment and limits ad content to a general-audience rating for all users.

For information about Google's data practices, see Google's advertising privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is designed for students, including children, and does not ask users to provide their age. To account for this audience, child-directed age treatment and general-audience ad content settings are applied to all ad requests.

Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell personal information or operate a server that receives calculation history. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its partners as described above. Reasonable measures are used to protect information stored by the app, but no electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its advertising integrations, or applicable requirements change. The latest version should be published at the privacy-policy URL shown on the app's store listing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact sraghep321@gmail.com.
