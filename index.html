Privacy Policy — Kenzy Calc

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Kenzy Calc is an educational scientific calculator intended for users aged 13 and older. It includes tools covering introductory, preparatory, and secondary study topics for review and learning.

Information stored on your device

The app stores calculation history locally on your device. It is not sent to the developer's servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its application data may delete this information.

The adult BMI tool processes the measurements you enter on your device. These measurements are not sent to the developer or advertising providers and are not saved by the app.

Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob on compatible devices and Huawei Ads Kit on compatible Huawei devices to display banner ads. The selected advertising provider and its partners may process information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, app and device details, and ad interaction data to provide and measure ads, subject to applicable settings and requirements. Google ad requests apply teen age-restricted treatment and a general-audience content rating.

For information about Google's data practices, see Google's advertising privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Huawei advertising data practices are described in Huawei's Privacy Statement.

Adult BMI tool

The BMI tool is intended only for adults aged 18 and older. Height and weight entered in the tool are processed on your device and are not stored or transmitted. BMI is a general screening estimate, not a medical diagnosis.

Age and intended audience

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not ask users to provide their age. Ad requests use teen age treatment and general-audience ad content settings.

Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell personal information or operate a server that receives calculation history. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google, Huawei, and their partners as described above. Reasonable measures are used to protect information stored by the app, but no electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its advertising integrations, or applicable requirements change. The latest version should be published at the privacy-policy URL shown on the app's store listing.
