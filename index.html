Privacy Policy

App: Kenzy Calc

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Kenzy Calc handles information when you use the calculator application.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Kenzy Calc may store your calculation history locally on your device using local storage. This information is used to provide the calculation history feature.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

The current version of Kenzy Calc does not require account registration. The app's calculation history is stored locally. This statement does not cover data that may be processed by third-party services added in future versions.

4. Advertising

Advertising services may be introduced in future versions of Kenzy Calc. If advertising is added, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant advertising providers, data practices, and available privacy choices.

5. Children's Privacy

Kenzy Calc does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The app's target audience and any applicable child-safety requirements will be addressed according to the app's distribution settings and applicable policies.

6. Data Security and Retention

Calculation history stored locally remains on the device unless removed by the user or deleted through app data management or uninstallation. Device backups and operating-system behavior may affect data retention.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
