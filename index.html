Privacy Policy — Kenzy Calc Last updated: October 23, 2026 Kenzy Calc ("the App") is a calculator application developed to provide basic calculation features. 1. Information We Collect Kenzy Calc stores calculation history locally on your device using local application storage. The App developer does not directly collect or store personal information through the calculator features. 2. Google AdMob and its partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, and improve advertising, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads 3. Local Storage Calculation history is stored on your device. The App does not send this history to the developer's servers. Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data may delete locally stored history. 4. Data Sharing The developer does not sell users' personal information. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google and its partners as described in Google's privacy documentation. 5. Children's Privacy The App is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Advertising settings and any applicable requirements for child-directed users must be configured appropriately. 6. Security Reasonable measures are taken to keep the App's locally stored information within the device. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. 7. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, advertising integrations, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page. 8. Contact For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the App developer through the contact details provided on the App's official listing, when available.
